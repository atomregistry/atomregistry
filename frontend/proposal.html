● The Problem

⚠ Cosmos Hub Has No Naming Layer

Every major blockchain ecosystem has a naming layer. ENS on Ethereum. SNS on Solana. Unstoppable Domains on Polygon. These are not optional niceties — they are foundational infrastructure that makes wallets readable, websites browsable, and identities portable.

Cosmos Hub has none. For a blockchain that calls itself the Internet of Blockchains, this is a conspicuous and consequential gap.

● Why Here

◆ Why This Belongs on Cosmos Hub

Not just .atom — any TLD

ENS gives you .eth. One TLD. Controlled by one organization. Atom Registry is a universal namespace marketplace. Anyone can own any top-level domain. Any word. Permanently. The owner sets the price, sets the policy, and earns 100% of all registration fees under their namespace forever. No ICANN. No registrar. No expiry.

Keplr integration — the switch

A lightweight Keplr update integrating Atom Registry name resolution instantly makes Web3 browsing available to millions of existing users simultaneously. Not a gradual adoption curve. A switch. The moment Keplr resolves .atom names natively, Keplr becomes a browser as well as a wallet.

● Commitments

◆ What Atom Registry Commits To

1. Full Open-Source Publication

All eight CosmWasm contracts, all browser tools, all deployment scripts — published to github.com/atomregistry/atomregistry under an open-source license within 30 days of proposal passage.

2. Community Governance — 2-of-3 Multisig

Atom Registry transitions to a 2-of-3 multisig governance structure. This is not a courtesy arrangement. It is enforced by math.

🔑 Founder Key Permanent · Required
👥 Community Key A Elected by Hub Gov
👥 Community Key B Elected by Hub Gov
Community A + Community B✗ No threshold. Nothing executes.
Founder + Community A✓ Executes
Founder + Community B✓ Executes
Founder alone✗ No threshold. Nothing executes.

The founder's role as Builder and Technical Lead is permanent by structural design. It cannot be altered, revoked, or voted away without the founder's own signature.

3. 50% of Platform Earnings to Cosmos LP — One Year

All platform earnings split 50/50 for one year from proposal passage. The community's 50% goes into a designated Cosmos Hub liquidity pool on Osmosis (specific pool determined by community governance vote prior to contract migration).

After one year: LP principal contributed on behalf of the founder is returned interest-free. Yield generated during the year remains in the pool as a permanent contribution to Cosmos liquidity. Default split after year one: 50% founder / 50% community pool unless governance proposes otherwise.

4. Security Audit

The Atom Registry contracts were developed using rigorous internal methodology built on the author's CosmWasm development expertise and smart contract security experience. Standard security practices — invariant-driven design, authorization validation on every entrypoint, overflow-checked arithmetic, reentrancy-safe state management, and adversarial path simulation — were applied throughout.

No formal audit from an outside agency has been performed at this time. If the community conditions acceptance of this proposal on a rigorous third-party audit of the new contracts, that audit will be conducted at the community's expense. The codebase will be published to github.com/atomregistry/atomregistry immediately upon proposal passage, enabling full independent community review in the interim.
