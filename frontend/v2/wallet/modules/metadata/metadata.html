Public metadata safety

Public fields can be read by anyone and may appear in wallets, explorers and search results.

  • Do not publish seed phrases, private keys, passwords or recovery words.
  • Avoid sensitive personal documents or private contact information.
  • Use public links and short profile text designed to be displayed by apps.
