Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-15

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Atom Registry handles information when you access the site, connect a wallet, view domain data, manage records, or interact with public Cosmos Hub smart contracts through the interface. Atom Registry is designed as a static frontend and does not require a traditional backend account, password, or server-side user profile for core use.

Static interface

The core site operates as a client-side web interface. This means most interactions happen directly in your browser, through your wallet, and through public blockchain infrastructure. The site itself does not create user accounts, custody private keys, store seed phrases, or maintain a private user database for ordinary interface activity.

Wallet information

When you connect a wallet, the browser may receive your public wallet address, selected account, wallet provider details, chain information, permissions, and connection status. This information is used to display ownership, balances, domain records, transaction prompts, marketplace actions, and account-specific interface states. Public wallet addresses and on-chain activity are not private by nature because they may be visible on public blockchain networks.

On-chain data

Domain registrations, TLD ownership, transfers, listings, resolver records, metadata, profile fields, dSSL records, transaction history, and related smart contract activity may be public blockchain data. Information submitted to public smart contracts may become permanently visible, searchable, indexable, and difficult or impossible to remove.

Local browser storage

The site may use localStorage, sessionStorage, or similar browser features to store non-sensitive interface preferences such as selected language, recent searches, selected wallet adapter, cached UI state, display preferences, dismissed notices, or recently viewed names. This data remains in your browser unless you clear it or use browser settings that remove site data automatically.

RPC, REST, and third-party infrastructure

Queries for balances, contract state, domain availability, names, listings, metadata, dSSL records, profiles, and resolver data may be sent from your browser to public Cosmos Hub RPC, REST, indexer, explorer, gateway, or other infrastructure providers. These external operators may receive technical request information such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, requested endpoints, and query parameters according to their own policies.

Wallets and external services

Wallet providers, explorers, repositories, public gateways, analytics services if enabled, and linked ecosystem tools are operated independently from Atom Registry. Your use of those services may be governed by separate privacy policies, permissions, telemetry settings, and data handling practices. You should review those policies before relying on third-party services.

Analytics and cookies

The static codebase does not require analytics cookies for core functionality. If analytics, error monitoring, performance tools, or similar services are added to a deployed version of the site, those services may process technical usage data according to their own terms and privacy policies. Any such integration should be reviewed in the deployed environment.

No sale of personal data

Atom Registry does not sell personal data through the static interface. The project does not use private wallet keys, seed phrases, or wallet signatures for advertising resale. Public blockchain information, however, may be accessed, analyzed, indexed, or reused by third parties because it exists on public networks.

Security

You are responsible for protecting your wallet, device, browser profile, seed phrase, hardware wallet, and private keys. Atom Registry will never need your seed phrase or private key. You should verify URLs, transaction prompts, contract addresses, wallet permissions, and external links before taking action.

Clearing local data

You can remove locally stored Atom Registry data by clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings or by manually clearing localStorage and sessionStorage through developer tools. Clearing local data does not remove public blockchain data or information already submitted to third-party services.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the project, interface, infrastructure, or supported features evolve. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you acknowledge the updated policy.

Contact

Privacy, security, or project-related questions may be submitted through the official communication channels made available by Atom Registry. Always verify that a communication channel is authentic before sharing information or taking action.
